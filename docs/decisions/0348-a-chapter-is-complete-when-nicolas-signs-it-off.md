# A chapter is complete when Nicolas signs it off

A chapter's `status:` is `planned` (a seed), `active` (built and still being made), or
`complete`. Nicolas sets `complete`; no gate or session infers it, because a balance lock or
a green suite is not the chapter being finished (ch07 is balance-locked with its ending
unbuilt). The wiki reads it (`gen_wiki.chapter_status`): an unfinished chapter's page opens
with a notice in `Template:Stub`'s shape, and the chapter list carries the balance lock in a
column of its own. Every check that held `active` chapters to a rule holds `complete` ones too.
_Decided: 2026-10-15 (Nicolas asked that unfinished chapters say so)_
