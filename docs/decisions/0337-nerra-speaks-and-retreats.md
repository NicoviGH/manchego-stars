# Nerra speaks in battle, and retreats instead of dying

Nicolas, 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10. ch06's merfolk elder had no face and no lines, so engaging
her played nothing and her death was silent (ADR 0263). She now has both of the boss quotes
vanilla gives Novala in the same seats, and nothing more: the cutscenes still belong to Messie.

- **Taunt (0x9EF's seat, two boxes):** "She said the fishermen were ours to take." / "Instead,
  Bremen sends this diverse bunch. How generous." Nicolas's wording. "She" stays unnamed: the
  Frostmaiden's plan is held for Messie's farewell in ch07 (ADR 0332).
- **Defeat (0x9F0's seat, one box) is a RETREAT:** "Ngh... Not today. The lake will have you
  another time..." She speaks for herself, not for the merfolk. Mechanically it is still FE8's
  defeat quote and the DefeatBoss win fires on it; Messie's scene never says she died. She
  survives, so ch07 (#27) may bring her back.
- **Face:** Freefall's Goth Serra, repainted in the merfolk battle anim's own colours (rust hair,
  pale green skin, gold eyes, the pink of their waist band for her top). The merfolk wear twin
  tails, so the bust had to (Nicolas). No merfolk mug exists on the FE-Repo or FEUniverse.
  Freefall grants F2U and F2E with credit.

Boss quotes render through one helper, `inject.scenes.boss_quote_message`, which ch05's Ravisin
also uses: a chapter names the trigger, speaker, face, message and locked box count.
