# FE8 class mix by phase: fliers, healers, a thief, the dancer, axes, few armors, Bishops late

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0005's table; "agree" counts independent sources.

**Early (Prologue-Ch8, shared route).**
- Lord (Eirika) and Seth, used carefully.
- One or two cavaliers (Franz).
- One flier (Vanessa), for villages and rescues.
- One healer (Moulder, then Natasha).
- One axe unit (Ross or Garcia) and possibly a wall (Gilliam).
- One mage (Lute and/or Artur).
- One thief (Colm), for chests and villages.
- Sources: R-1ST13, GF-WCSIU, GF-QUICK, SF-COMP, GF-NOTOWER teams.
- Specific needs mentioned:
  - Armour-killer access.
  - Hammers and Horseslayers carried by the enemy are a reason to prefer Paladin over Great
    Knight (YT Gail Forest).
  - Bring a Vulnerary for Seth against Horseslayers (SF-SETH).
  - Ch5 and 5x need Orson as bait (YT tier list).

**Mid (Ch9-15, route split).**
- Add the route's strong joiners:
  - Eirika route: Tana, Innes, Gerik, Tethys, Saleh, L'Arachel and Dozla, Cormag at Ch13.
  - Ephraim route: Kyle, Forde, Amelia, Duessel, Cormag, Knoll, Tana.
- **Field the dancer as soon as Tethys joins** (7 sources).
- **Promote around level 10-15** using whatever items are available (see Q4).
- A thief still matters, because chests continue until about Ch16-17 (R-WHO).
- Specific needs:
  - Restore staves for Ephraim Ch14's status staves (R-1ST25).
  - Ephraim route Ch11 and Ch14 are the hardest maps (R-1ST25).

**Late (Ch16-Final, monster-heavy).**
- **Light magic and Bishop's Slayer.** Artur, and Moulder or Natasha if made Bishops, are the
  monster killers.
  - IGN Ch18: Gorgons and the Gorgon boss should go to Artur as a Bishop.
  - FEW Artur: Bishop is better because of the monster density.
  - SF-COMP: feed monsters to Artur.
  - R-1ST13: lots of dark magic late, so level Artur and Natasha into Bishops.
  - Agree: 5.
- **Mages generally** for wyverns and high-defence monsters (R-WHO).
- **High movement: fliers and Warp users** (R-WHO: the last stretch is about movement).
- **Lords' Sieglinde and Siegmund, and the legendary weapons** (Audhulma, Vidofnir, Garm,
  Excalibur, Ivaldi, Latona, Nidhogg, Gleipnir). GF-WCSIU builds its whole team so that one
  unit of each weapon type exists to wield one.
- **Myrrh** for bosses (GF-WCSIU, NL).
- **The final map requires 12 units with both lords** (GF-QUICK). This is why late joiners
  and filler get dusted off.
- One poster's usual final party (NL):
  - Eirika, Ephraim and Tethys.
  - A healer (usually L'Arachel) and a tank (usually Amelia).
  - A magic user (Lute or Ewan).
  - A high-Strength unit (Ross or Gerik).
  - A flier.
