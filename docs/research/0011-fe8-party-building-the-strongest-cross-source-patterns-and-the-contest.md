# FE8 party building: the strongest cross-source patterns and the contested points

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0005's table; "agree" counts independent sources.

## Strongest cross-source patterns

1. Seth is always used, held back from regular kills early, and given bosses (6+ sources).
2. The core is Eirika, Seth, Franz, Vanessa (or Tana), Moulder, Artur and/or Lute, Colm, plus
   Tethys, Gerik, Cormag and Saleh once they join (5+ sources each).
3. Field one flier, one or two healers, one thief, one dancer, one or two axe users, and
   light magic for late monsters (5-7 sources).
4. Duplicates are resolved by availability plus stats at the moment of choice. The earlier
   unit (Franz, Vanessa, Moulder) usually wins unless the later one is clearly better (Innes
   over Neimi).
5. Promotions mostly favour movement: Paladin for Forde and Franz, Ranger, Mage Knight,
   Cavalier!Amelia. The exceptions are Bishop for the anti-monster role and Hero for Gerik and
   Garcia's hand axes.
6. The team is about 10-12 strong, finalised around Ch15-17. The 12-unit final map forces
   filler.

## Contested points

- How much Seth should kill (measured vs "let him carry").
- Vanessa vs Tana; and Wyvern Knight vs Falcoknight (forums split roughly evenly).
- Franz Paladin vs Great Knight (SF favours Paladin; GameFAQs 2013 posters favoured GK).
- Artur: Bishop (guides, wiki, reddit) vs Sage (veteran forum posters).
- Gilliam, Lute, L'Arachel and Tethys's value.
- Neimi vs Innes depends on whether you grind.
- Tower of Valni: avoid entirely, use occasionally, or abuse.
- Promote at 10-15 vs at 20.
