# FE8 per-chapter deploy: where the sources disagree

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0012's table.

1. **Seth's role early:** bench/minimise (SH, ZA, LP) vs use freely, especially on bosses (KE; FEW
   recommends promoted units for 11A/11B).
2. **Ross in ch3-4:** feed him everything to reach level 10 by ch4 (SH) vs he is a liability unless
   levelled (SW) vs bench candidate (KE).
3. **Neimi:** bench from ch3 (KE, SH, FEW-char) vs level her (ZA) vs probation (LP).
4. **Franz in ch6 fog:** bench (LP, because of Halberd/Horseslayer) vs use him to rush the Halberd soldier
   (FEW) or as lance bait (KE).
5. **Cavalry in ch15 sand:** leave them home (SH) vs still effective on the grass fronts (FEW) vs don't
   worry (SW).
6. **Fliers in 13B:** leave pegasi/wyverns home (SH) vs bring flyers (LR).
7. **Thief in the Final chapter:** bring one (SW, SH) vs unnecessary (KE, FEW).
8. **Myrrh in ch16:** level her now (LR, KE, ZA) vs save her (SH).
9. **Natasha/Joshua long-term:** KE advises against both; most others field Natasha as the healer.
10. **Deploy counts:** LR is often one lower than SF/SW; ch19 is 17 (SF, LR) or 19 (KE).
11. **Promotions genuinely split:** Gilliam (General vs GK), Amelia tier 2 (Paladin vs GK), Ewan tier 2
    (Summoner vs Mage Knight vs Sage), Tana/Vanessa (Wyvern Knight vs Falcoknight), Colm (Rogue vs
    Assassin), Cormag (Wyvern Lord vs Wyvern Knight), Artur (Bishop majority, Sage minority).
