# FE8 deploy rules of thumb across chapters, and the deploy count per chapter

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0012's table.

1. **Preparations / deploy cap.** No unit choice Prologue-Ch3: every unit you have fights (SW ch2: "All
   (8 in total)"; SW ch3: "All"). The first Preparations screen is **Chapter 4** (SW ch4: "This is your
   first chapter with the preparations screen"; SF, LR also give their first counts at ch4). From then on
   the cap is roughly 9-12, jumping for ch19-20 (see table).
2. **Seth is the safety net, not the EXP sink early.** SH (Prologue: "Don't use Seth because Eirika needs
   the EXP"), ZA ("he's a Jeigan anyways so you don't want him fighting unless you absolutely need to"),
   LP (benches Seth in ch4, "too awesome to use right now"). **CONTESTED:** KE says the opposite: Seth is
   "considered the best unit in the game", use him to kill every early boss, and from ch9 "Treat him like
   any other unit." FEW ch11A and ch11B also "highly recommend" promoted units such as Seth.
3. **A thief rides along whenever there are chests, doors or stealables; benched otherwise.** KE ("just
   deploy Colm as long as there's some treasure to steal and bench him otherwise"). LR, ZA, SH, SW, FEW
   all name Colm (later Rennac) on ch6, 7, 8, 11A, 14, 16, 19, Final. Two thieves in ch19 (FEW, KE, ZA).
   Contrary voice: SW ch4/ch5 ("Colm can't do much for you here", "Colm isn't very useful"), LP benches
   Colm from ch9 ("underachieving severely").
4. **Fog chapters bring a thief (vision) and Torch users.** Fog of war in ch6, 11A, 11B, 19 (FEW). LR,
   SW, KE, LP, ZA, SH all say Colm for vision and/or Torch staff on Natasha/Moulder.
5. **Monster chapters favour light magic (Artur, later Bishops with Slayer), and Sacred Twin weapons at
   the end.** Ch4 (FEW, SW, LP, KE), 11A/12A (LR: "a Bishop is your best choice here"; SH ch12A "Bring a
   bishop along"), 18 (SF: light magic vs Gorgons), 20/Final (SF, KE, SW, FEW).
6. **Healer always deployed.** Every walkthrough that lists a party lists at least one staff user (LR
   names Natasha in ~10 chapters; ZA "a healer or two"; KE "It's a good idea to have a staff user").
   Restore staff specifically for status-staff chapters (9B first; 14A/14B Berserk; 18 Stone).
7. **Fliers for objectives** (villages, rescues, civilians, eggs): Vanessa ch2/ch4/ch6, Tana/Vanessa ch9A,
   10A, 17, 18 (all sources). KE: "You need at least one flier... initially using both and then committing
   to whichever one has better stats and benching the other sometime during Act 2."
8. **Archers are the first benched class.** KE benches Neimi from ch3 ("Archers cannot retaliate against
   1-ranged weapons"), SH ("Trash this girl", "I wouldn't use Neimi here. She sucks"), FEW-char ("a poor
   choice on Eirika's route"), LP keeps her on probation. Only ZA defends her ("a better archer than
   Hineas [Innes], hands down"). Gilliam is the second most-benched (KE: use him to tank early, "When he
   is no longer useful, bench him"; FEW-char: "most players will find many other units more useful than
   Gilliam after the early-game"), but he is the go-to ballista/archer sponge in ch7 and ch10A (SH, SW, LP).
9. **Recruit talkers drive deployment.** Eirika is always there; the non-lord talkers a player must bring:
   Neimi (Colm, ch3), Natasha (Joshua, ch5, forced anyway), Ross (Garcia, ch2), Franz (Amelia, 9A/9B/13A),
   Tana (Innes 10A, Cormag 10B, Syrene 17), Gerik/Innes (Tethys/Gerik/Marisa, 10A, they start on map),
   L'Arachel (Dozla 11; Rennac 14 for free), Saleh (Ewan, 12A, forced), Ewan (Marisa, 12B), Duessel
   (Cormag, 10B), Vanessa/Tana/Innes (Syrene, 17).
10. **Deployment order matters vs status staves** (single source, FEW 9B and 14B): enemy AI "prefers to
   target characters at the bottom of the unit list" with Sleep/Berserk, so put resistant units last.
