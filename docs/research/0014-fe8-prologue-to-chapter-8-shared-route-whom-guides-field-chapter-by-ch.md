# FE8 Prologue to Chapter 8 (shared route): whom guides field, chapter by chapter

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0012's table.

## Prologue: The Fall of Renais
- No prep; Eirika + Seth only (FEW, SF).
- Let Eirika take the EXP; Seth only if needed (SH, ZA). **CONTESTED:** KE has Seth kill the boss with
  the Steel Sword ("Seth gets good experience from bosses") and says to aim for Seth's S rank in swords.

## Chapter 1: Escape!
- No prep. Franz (Cavalier) and Gilliam (Knight) join on T2 (FEW, LR).
- Give Eirika and Franz the kills; Eirika is good against Fighters by weapon triangle (ZA, FEW).
- Gilliam: tank and soften so Franz/Eirika finish (KE: "When he is no longer useful, bench him").
- Boss Breguet (armoured Knight): Eirika's Rapier (effective vs armour) or Seth's Silver Lance (FEW, ZA,
  LR, KE).

## Chapter 2: The Protected
- No prep: everyone fights (SW "All (8 in total)").
- Vanessa rescues Ross from the peaks; Moulder heals him; Eirika talks to Ross; Ross talks to Garcia
  (SW, SH, LR, ZA, FEW). Ross and Garcia also join automatically at the end if both survive (FEW).
- Seth or Franz (sword equipped) rides to the southern village for the Pure Water before bandits raze it
  (SW). Javelins for Franz, Gilliam, Seth, Vanessa (FEW).
- Feed Ross the kills, including boss Bone (SW). Vanessa is poor against Bone (SW, single source).

## Chapter 3: The Bandits of Borgo
- No prep ("All", SW). Neimi joins on T1; Neimi talks to Colm when he appears end of T1/T2 (all sources).
- Keep Gilliam and Vanessa away from boss Bazba: "very low chance of hitting" (SW, single source).
- Ross takes all EXP except the boss; Moulder heals (SH). ZA instead says "use Corma [Colm] and Naemi
  [Neimi] ... level them up". **CONTESTED (who to feed):** SH Ross; ZA Colm+Neimi; KE starts benching
  Neimi here.
- Garcia, Neimi or javelin users vs the archer and hand-axe Brigand (FEW).

## Chapter 4: Ancient Horrors (first Preparations)
- 9 slots, Artur forced (SF, LR, SW, FEW). All-monster map: Bonewalkers, Revenants, Mogalls, boss Entombed.
- **Field:** Artur (light magic, effective vs monsters and Mogalls' dark magic) (FEW, SW, KE, LP). Lute
  (recruit by visiting the southern village; Vanessa flies there) (SH, LP, FEW).
- **Bench:** KE "Bench Neimi and either Ross, Colm, or Garcia." SW: "Colm can't do much for you here",
  "Ross may be a liability if he isn't significantly leveled up". LP benches Seth.
- **CONTESTED (Ross):** SH wants Ross fielded to reach level 10 here and promote; SW calls him a
  liability unless levelled; KE lists him as a bench candidate.
- ZA: "bring along your wimpy units", with medicine. FEW: keep units on forest tiles.

## Chapter 5: The Empire's Reach
- 9 slots, Natasha forced (SF, SW, FEW, LR). First Arena and first shop+armoury combo (FEW).
- Natasha talks to Joshua (all sources).
- **Bench:** KE "benching Neimi, Colm, Gilliam, and either Garcia or Ross". SW "Colm isn't very useful".
  ZA would not put Gilliam in the arena ("Armors are at a pretty high disadvantage") but does send him
  solo up the west path.
- Brigand reinforcements appear near the start: keep squishy units guarded (FEW).
- **Promotion point:** Ross reaches level 10 around here. SH and LP: **Pirate** (SH: "His dad, Garcia, is
  already a fighter... Pirate can also cross water"; LP: Pirate is the only chance at the class). See
  promotion table.
- KE does not recommend Natasha or Joshua long-term ("I don't recommend using her despite her higher
  growth rates", Joshua "I don't recommend using him long-term"). Others use both (LR names Natasha
  repeatedly; FEW-char gives Joshua a Swordmaster path).

## Chapter 5x: Unbroken Heart (Ephraim interlude)
- No choice: Ephraim, Forde, Kyle, Orson all forced (FEW).
- **Orson leaves at chapter end (traitor):** strip his items; do not give him kills/boss EXP (FEW, LR
  "NEVER USE ORSON!", SH "Do NOT use Orson"). KE is softer: "Feel free to use him if it's helpful".
- Forde and Kyle do the fighting (KE, SH, FEW); Forde's Javelin then Ephraim's Reginleif on Zonta (FEW).
  If Forde or Kyle fall here they return in ch8 (SF, FEW).

## Chapter 6: Victims of War
- 10 slots, Eirika forced; **fog of war** (FEW); enemy Bael spider hunts 3 civilians (reaches them about
  T7-8) (SF, FEW).
- **Field:** Vanessa (fly east to protect civilians/kill Bael) (SF, SW, LR, ZA, KE, FEW, LP). Colm for
  vision in fog (ZA, SH, SW, KE, LP). Natasha or Moulder with Torch staff (LR, SW, KE). Gilliam as a wall
  (SH, LP). Seth patrols the west against a 2-Cavalier pincer (KE).
- **Avoid / careful:** mounted units — a Fighter has a Horseslayer and a Soldier a Halberd (SW "Try not
  to sent a mounted unit up too far", ZA, LR "some of them are equipped w/ anti-horse wpns"). LP benched
  Franz for exactly this reason. FEW instead says have Franz and/or Seth rush the Halberd soldier before
  he acts. **CONTESTED (Franz):** bench (LP) vs use to kill the Halberd unit (FEW) vs use with a lance
  as bait (KE).
- Keep fragile units back; hide healer in forest (SW, SH). Don't frontline level-capped units (SH).

## Chapter 7: Waterside Renvall
- 10 slots; ballistae; boss Murray (Cavalier) drops a Knight Crest (SW, SF).
- **Field:** Colm to steal the Energy Ring from a mage (LR, ZA, SW, FEW, KE). Gilliam or Seth to soak
  ballista shots (SW, SH). Artur and Lute behind 2-3 front-liners (SW, ZA). Vanessa north / to rescue the
  ballista archer (SW, ZA).
- Vanessa is the ballista's favourite target: send someone else at it (FEW).
- **CONTESTED (Neimi):** SW sends Neimi north to man the friendly ballista; SH "I wouldn't use Neimi
  here. She sucks".
- Boss: ranged magic/bows then Eirika's Rapier (FEW), or lance/hand axe and let him attack you (SW).
- **Promotion point:** Knight Crest here; KE spends it on Franz at level 10-15 ("don't delay the
  promotion too long").

## Chapter 8: It's a Trap!
- 9 slots; armoured Knights, archers, shamans; boss Tirado (General). Ephraim, Forde, Kyle join T2 (FEW).
- **Field:** Colm for chests (LR, ZA, SH, FEW: Silver Sword, Elysian Whip). Artur kills the Shaman
  through the wall (SW); Ross, Lute, Artur through the wall (FEW). Franz and Gilliam vs knights and
  mercenaries (FEW). Armour-room party: Seth, Gilliam, Artur, Ross, Lute (FEW). LR: "Bring Colm n' all
  your strongest chars!"
- Avoid: "Don't use Ross unless he's upgraded" in the armour room (SW). (LP did send Ross and he cleared
  three knights unharmed.)
- Tirado: Armorslayer (Seth), Rapier, Reginleif (SW, SH, KE, FEW).
- **Promotion point:** Elysian Whip (flier promotion) obtained; KE: whip Vanessa/Tana to Wyvern Knight
  "as early as Chapter 8 at level 10-15".
