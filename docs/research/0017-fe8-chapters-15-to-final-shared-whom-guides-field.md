# FE8 Chapters 15 to Final (shared): whom guides field

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0012's table.

## Chapter 15: Scorched Sand
- 12 slots. Desert. Bosses Valter (Wyvern Knight) and Caellach (Hero). Wyverns, Pegasi, Cavaliers,
  Shamans. On Eirika's route Ephraim, Duessel, Knoll arrive end of T2 (FEW, SF).
- **Field:** mages and fliers, unslowed by sand (FEW "As with all desert chapters...", SH, SW). A thief/
  rogue for buried treasure (LR, ZA). Anti-horse and anti-flier weapons, Cormag with Dragonspear (LR).
  Axes vs Valter/wyverns, lances/swords vs Caellach (SH). Bow users (SW: two dedicated bows, Warriors with
  Longbows vs Valter). High-HP/DEF walls: Generals, Great Knights, Heroes (SW). One Bishop for heal +
  attack (SW). Fly a healer or wyverns over to the twin's group (ZA).
- **CONTESTED (cavalry):** SH "Bring less or no horse-riders" (both routes). FEW: "Despite Seth's claims
  in the intro, Cavalry still remain quite effective on this level" because the southern front and T8
  reinforcements are on grass; field Duessel, promoted cavaliers, Mage Knight Lute. SW: "don't worry about
  whether your men are on horses or not."
- KE: pick Saleh or Lute, bench the other, in 12-slot chapters.

## Chapter 16: Ruled by Madness
- 12 slots (LR 11). Myrrh joins "Automatically from preparations" (FEW). Great Knights, Cavaliers,
  Valkyrie, Sages with Purge, Druids, Berserk staff, thieves on T6/T16 (SF, FEW, SH, LR).
- **Field:** a thief/Rennac for chests before the enemy thieves (SH, ZA, LR). Myrrh (LR "Best chance to
  LvUp Myrrh!", ZA, KE "most tanky unit on your roster"). Healer (LR Natasha). L'Arachel (ZA). A small
  detachment for the Berserk bishop (FEW). Magic user at 2 range vs Orson if he has a Runesword (FEW);
  axes vs Orson (SH).
- **CONTESTED (Myrrh):** SH "Save Myrrh for the final battles" (Ephraim route) vs LR/KE/ZA field and
  level her now. LP used her sparingly (Dragonstone has 50 uses).
- **Promotion point:** Lunar/Solar Brace after this chapter; KE "promote both immediately". LP waited
  until level 20.

## Chapter 17: River of Regrets
- 12 slots; objective defeat Lyon. Wyvern Riders every other turn, Druids, Cavaliers, late Paladin/Great
  Knight; civilians to save; Syrene recruitable (Vanessa, Tana or Innes) (FEW, SF).
- **Field:** fliers to save civilians and recruit Syrene (LR, ZA, SH, SW). Mounted units (LR, SW: "send
  your flying and horseback units to rush Lyon"). A thief to steal a Dragonshield (ZA). Pegasus with
  javelin to interrupt mages (FEW). Non-fliers get mountain defence in the west (FEW). SH: Tana or a
  Pirate/Berserker (water).
- LP: Syrene is mainly a Triangle Attack piece; skip her if Tana/Vanessa are regulars.

## Chapter 18: Two Faces of Evil
- 12 slots (LR 10). Gorgons, Gorgon Eggs, Mogall, Gargoyle, Bael; lava; Stone status (FEW).
- **Field:** fliers/high-movement units to smash eggs (50 EXP each) (FEW, SF, ZA, LR, SW). Restore staves
  for Stone (FEW, SF). High-avoid units or light magic vs Gorgons (SF). Weak units for egg EXP, strong for
  hatched Gorgons (SW, LR). A longbow user for eggs on cliffs (LP Neimi).
- **Bench:** thief ("none of the enemies will drop items", ZA). KE: don't deploy Tethys unless Knoll's
  phantoms bait the long-range spells ("Tethys can get 1-shot"). Shadowshot Gorgon threatens weak units
  (FEW). Fliers cannot cross the abyss (FEW).

## Chapter 19: Last Hope
- 17 slots (SF, LR; KE 19). Defend Mansel 13 turns or kill Riev. **Fog of war** (FEW). Mostly physical
  promoted enemies; Snipers from the NE.
- **Field:** two thieves, Colm AND Rennac, for both chest sides and the secret shop (FEW, KE, ZA, LR).
  Durable/high-DEF units to plug chokepoints (FEW, SW, KE "2 of your most tanky units using Javelins",
  SH "Only bring strong characters"). All staff users + Torches (KE), 2 healers (ZA), Saleh (FEW). Fliers
  with a Fili Shield (FEW, KE Cormag). Innes at 2 range (FEW). Seth as taxi (FEW). Knoll's phantoms (FEW).
  LR adds L'Arachel, Myrrh, cavalry.

## Chapter 20: Darkling Woods
- 18 slots. Monsters (Cyclops, Maelduin, Deathgoyle, Elder Bael, Gorgons, Arch Mogall); bosses Riev
  (moves) and Morva (Manakete) (SF, SH, FEW).
- **Field:** Myrrh, to level her for the final boss (SW, KE, LR, LP). Artur/light magic and Sacred Twin
  users (KE, LR). Fliers and cavalry (LR). Iron weapons for the swarm (ZA). No thief needed (ZA); grab two
  Chest Keys so the next chapter needs no thief (FEW). FEW: units meant for Fomortiis need about 51
  physical bulk and 23 Speed; only high HP survives Morva.
- SH: "Only bring strong characters... The only characters that you should use are Eirika, Ephraim, and
  Seth" (outlier).

## Final: Sacred Stone (parts 1 and 2)
- 12 slots; Draco Zombies, Gorgons (Shadowshot), Arch Mogalls, Deathgoyles; Lyon then Fomortiis
  (SF, SW, FEW). Part 2 carries over the part-1 party (SF).
- **Field:** every Sacred Twin weapon wielder (SF, KE, SW "at least six characters with sacred weapons"),
  Myrrh (SF, KE, LR, SW). Bows and Wyrmslayer vs Draco Zombies (SF). Tethys "even if you don't normally
  use her" for an extra T1 hit on the final boss (FEW, single source). Two healers (SW). High raw HP vs
  Draco Zombies, bait them away with a sturdy unit (SF, FEW). Purge/Bolting vs Shadowshot Gorgons (SW).
- **CONTESTED (thief):** SW and SH bring a thief/Rennac for the southern chests; KE: "you don't need to
  bring Rennac in this chapter since all the enemies drop the keys"; FEW: two Chest Keys from ch20 replace
  the thief.
