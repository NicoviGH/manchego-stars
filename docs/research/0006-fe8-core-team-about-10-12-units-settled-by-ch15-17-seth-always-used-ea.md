# FE8 core team: about 10-12 units settled by Ch15-17; Seth always used, early as support

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0005's table; "agree" counts independent sources.

### Team size

- **About 8-12 trained units. Late chapters let you field ~12+, and the deploy count, not the
  roster, is the binding limit.**
  - R-1ST13: train no more than about 12.
  - GF-WCSIU: says 12 roster slots.
  - GF-NOTOWER p6: "10 units + Tethys" is best; also mentions 10-12-man teams.
  - GF-QUICK: 8 is generally best. The last stage requires 12, and only the lords are
    mandatory.
  - GF-8v12: 3 posters pro-8, 1 pro-12, 1 says the game is too easy for it to matter. Only
    Ch19 really warrants filler.
  - GF-CG: 12-16 is "manageable", and its final party is 12.
  - R-WHO: a first-time player hit the 12 limit at Ch16 and had to bench people.
  - **Agree: 7 sources.** Efficiency-minded posters lean to 8, casual posters and guides to 12.
    A casual bot should target ~10-12 trained units, with the dancer and a thief as cheap
    extras.

### When the core forms

- **The Eirika-route opening (Ch1-8) is the formative stretch.** Players pick the core from
  Franz, Gilliam, Vanessa, Moulder, Ross, Garcia, Neimi, Colm, Artur, Lute, Natasha and Joshua.
- **Chapters 9-13 add route-specific joiners. Final team decisions happen around Ch15-17.**
  - R-WHO: the last recruit joins at Ch17, so that is the point to finalise the team.
  - R-1ST13: suggests Gilliam, Franz, Neimi and Joshua as the first core.
  - YT "Problem with Tana": the first eight chapters are basically a tutorial.
  - **Agree: 3.**

### Seth

- **Almost everyone uses him, all game. Nobody recommends benching him.**
  - SF-SETH: unanimous.
  - GF-SETH: 4 posters for using him, 1 against.
  - GF-WCSIU calls him the best unit.
  - R-snip 4rar2r says he could solo the game.
  - R-1ST25 advises relying on him.
  - SF-YATL and SF-COMP put him in a tier of his own.
  - **Agree: 6+.**
- **How casual players use him early (Prologue to ~Ch8-10).** Common advice:
  - Don't let him take regular kills.
  - Use him to rescue units, bait enemies and chip enemies for others to finish.
  - **Give him the boss kills.** Bosses give big EXP even to a promoted unit.
  - Sources: GF-SETH (Distant_Rainbow: chip, feed him a little via counters and bosses; My_Unit:
    weaken, let others finish), SF-SETH (Tables, ping, Paper Jam: bosses and a few kills),
    IGN "How to Level Up" (others take normal units, Seth takes the boss), R-1ST13 (strip his
    weapons and use him as a bait tank until he can be replaced), R-1ST25 (give him every
    boss kill except Ch1's), YT tier list (whittle enemies down to set up kills for the
    cavaliers). **Agree: 6.**
- **When the restraint stops.** GF-SETH: once he is around Paladin level 4-5 (about
  Ch13-14), he can fight freely without EXP worries. R-snip 1vyxefn: lean on Seth and later
  joiners. R-1ST13 (a minority view): replace him with your own units eventually. The
  efficient view (SF-SETH, GF-NOTOWER p12) is that he lives on boss kills alone and stays
  useful to the end.
- **Contested: how much to feed him.**
  - SF-SETH: a split between "let him solo" (4 posters) and "measured use" (2).
  - Casual first-timer threads lean measured (R-1ST13: 2 commenters).
  - A minority never use him (GF-SETH: 1; GF-NOTOWER p1 wilowns1).
