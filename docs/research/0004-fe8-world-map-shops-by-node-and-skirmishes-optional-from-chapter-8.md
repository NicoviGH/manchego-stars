# FE8 world map shops by node, and skirmishes optional from Chapter 8

- **World map shops** (<https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/World_map>,
  <https://serenesforest.net/the-sacred-stones/miscellaneous/shops/main-story/>): armories at
  Ide, Serafew, Port Kiris, Narube River, Bethroen, Rausten Court; vendors at Caer Pelyn,
  Taizel, Serafew, Port Kiris, Narube River, Bethroen, Jehanna Hall, Rausten Court; secret
  shops (Member Card) at Rausten Court, Grado Keep, Jehanna Hall.
- **Skirmishes** (monster bands) start after Chapter 8, respawn after each chapter or a Tower
  of Valni / Lagdou Ruins visit, at most three at once, and give EXP, gold and drops; a band
  blocks its node but can usually be routed around, so they are optional
  (<https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Skirmish>).
