# FE8 Ephraim's route, Chapters 9-14 (brief): whom guides field

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0012's table.

- **9B Fort Rigwald** (11 slots; Ephraim forced). Bring a thief (ZA, SH, LR Colm) and Franz or Ephraim
  for Amelia (SH, FEW). First enemy status staff: buy Restore (FEW). Don't move Tana (FEW, SH). ZA:
  "NOT fighting with Amelia in this level". LR: Natasha, Colm, Javelins for Vanessa and cavalry.
- **10B Turning Traitor** (11 slots; defend Duessel 10 turns). Mounted units for the southern pirates
  (FEW, ZA) and fliers (LR, ZA "Vanessa/Tahna"). Tana or Duessel talks to Cormag (FEW, LR); ZA says
  only Duessel. Speedy lance users on the isolated forest (FEW).
- **11B Phantom Ship** (11 slots; fog). Promoted units Seth, Duessel (FEW); Natasha, fliers, Bishop,
  Torches (LR); Colm for fog (ZA). Ephraim talks to L'Arachel, she talks to Dozla (all).
- **12B Landing at Taizel** (12 slots). Bishop, fliers, cavalry (LR); strong units (SH, ZA). Ewan visits
  the northern house, Ewan talks to Marisa; bait Marisa with an unarmed bulky unit (FEW, SH, ZA).
- **13B Fluorspar's Oath** (11 slots; rivers; Pegasus reinforcements; Selena with Bolting). 2-range
  high-RES/evade unit on a forest above Selena (FEW). **CONTESTED:** SH "Don't bring any pegasus or wyvern
  riders. Bring pirates or berserkers"; LR "Bring Colm, flyers n' Cavaliers"; FEW says the water shortcut
  for Dozla/Pirate Ross "is rarely especially useful".
- **14B Father and Son** (12 slots; LR "ONE OF THE HARDEST MAPS"). L'Arachel (Rennac, healing) (SH, LR,
  ZA); Restore users on both sides; deploy-order manipulation so high-RES units are targeted by Berserk
  (FEW); thief/rogue for doors (ZA, LR). Armorslayer or axes vs Vigarde (SH, LR).
