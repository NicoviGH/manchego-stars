# FE8 EXP habits: feed growing units, Seth softens, the Tower of Valni to catch up

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0005's table; "agree" counts independent sources.

- **Feed kills to the growth units you've committed to. Strong units (Seth, pre-promotes)
  weaken enemies, and growth units take the last hit.**
  - IGN How to Level Up: whittle enemies down with the strongest units, finish with the ones
    you want to level.
  - GF-SETH, SF-SETH, R-1ST13 (bait), YT tier list. Agree: 5.
- **Pre-promotes are auto-levelled. Concentrated EXP goes to 3-5 early growth units.**
  - GF-NOTOWER p12: only Eirika, Franz and Vanessa need early EXP. Saleh mostly needs weapon
    EXP. Gerik promotes when EXP dries up. Ephraim and Duessel are auto-levelled.
  - The same post argues adding Lute or Artur takes a big slice from Franz and Vanessa.
  - Agree: 2.
- **Healers and dancers level through staff and dance EXP.** Spam Heal, and buy cheap Torch or
  Barrier staves to get a healer EXP when everyone is at full HP. Use dancing and stealing
  whenever possible.
  - Sources: IGN, GF-HEAL (AudioAutomica: buy a dozen Torch staves), GF-8v12 (the dancer has
    an independent EXP pool), GF-NOTOWER p12 (a thief's bonus EXP doesn't starve others).
  - Agree: 4.
- **Bosses go to whoever benefits most.** Often Seth (big EXP even when promoted), or a
  growth unit when the boss is easy (R-1ST25: Ch1's boss to the cavalier).
- **Pet units and trainees.**
  - Ross is the trainee people baby (GF-TRAINEE: 81% of poll votes rank him first; R-1ST13:
    give Ross every kill he can survive; GF-NOTOWER p6).
  - Amelia and Ewan are trained mostly by trainee fans or via the Tower (R-1ST25: box in an
    archer and poke it until Amelia gets D lances, then javelins). Their late join makes it
    hard without grinding.
  - Agree: 4.
  - Neimi is the classic grind-dependent unit (SF-NEIMI: use Neimi only with the Tower, 3
    posters).
- **Tower of Valni and skirmishes. Contested, and this is the biggest split.**
  - **Unused or optional:** GF-TOWER 4 avoid or say optional vs 3 use. R-1ST13: 4 commenters
    say no grinding is needed. R-1ST25: avoid it for the intended challenge, but use it to
    rescue a trainee or recover from deaths. YT "Does the Tower ruin fun?": one pass between
    maps smooths the difficulty curve.
  - **Used to catch up a benched or weak unit, or after deaths:**
    - R-1ST13: one commenter used the first floor repeatedly to bring others up to snuff.
    - GF-QUICK SSJDennis: saves trainees for the Tower and adds them if they turn out good.
    - GF-SETH OP: plans a single Tower trip at Ch16.
    - YT (CDBookMan): after Tana and Ross died, went to the Tower to train replacements.
    - GF-TRAINQA: safe methods are a wall of strong units with weapons removed, or chipping
      the stationary Entombed boss with ranged weapons.
  - **Abused by some** (GF-TOWER schattenkreuz: 20/20 units; YT "Ruin fun": stayed until all
    promotable units hit 20).
  - **Practical reading for a "careful ordinary" bot:**
    - Mostly train on story maps.
    - Allow occasional skirmish or Tower trips to catch up a unit that fell behind, or a
      replacement after a death.
    - Don't fully grind the roster.
    - Agree that occasional catch-up is the casual norm: 5.
