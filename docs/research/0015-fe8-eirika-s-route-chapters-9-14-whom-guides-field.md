# FE8 Eirika's route, Chapters 9-14: whom guides field

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0012's table.

## Chapter 9A: Distant Blade
- 11 slots (SF, SW, LP; LR says 12). **Tana forced** (FEW). Pirates raid villages from T2; Amelia arrives
  T4-5 (SW T4, SF T5) and leaves at T11/12.
- **Field:** Franz (talks to Amelia; LP brought him "for convenience in recruiting", SH "Bring Franz",
  SW). A pegasus for the southern village (SF, LR, KE) — Vanessa/Tana or a Pirate Ross (SF, FEW). Mounted
  unit for the western village (SF names Seth, FEW). SW: "a VERY solid character like a paladin or great
  knight paired with a pegasus knight" plus a long-range attacker. LR: "Bring anti-sword wpns" (mercenary
  heavy, SH agrees).
- Eirika can talk to Amelia from outside her range (FEW). Colm can steal Amelia's Speedwing instead
  (FEW).
- Fliers beware archers near the western village (FEW, SF). Seth vs the Sniper on Difficult (FEW).
- Binks: Axereaver (from a nearby soldier), swords first then axes (FEW, SH).

## Chapter 10A: Revolt at Carcino
- 12 slots; arena usable (SF). Allies Innes, Gerik, Tethys start as NPCs; Marisa arrives as an enemy.
- **Field:** Tana to talk to Innes (SF: Eirika "takes a longer time with her lower movement"; LR, KE, SW).
  Natasha/healers (LR, SH "a lot of healing staves if you want to use the arena"). Seth or Tana to carry
  Eirika (SH). A knight to enter the gated room (SH). Gerik talks to Marisa (all). KE: Tethys "Always
  deploy her as a Dancer" from here.
- Fliers avoid ballista range (FEW). High-resistance unit or mage vs Pablo (Sage) (SW); Seth and Innes
  have the Luck to dodge his crits (FEW). Colm steals the Red Gem / Talisman (SW, KE).
- **Marisa:** FEW calls her "among the weakest units in the game"; "ignoring or killing her is a viable
  option".
- **CONTESTED (talker chain):** SF/LR/FEW: Innes → Gerik or Tethys → the other. SH: Innes → Tethys →
  Gerik. All agree Marisa is Gerik's.

## Chapter 11A: Creeping Darkness
- 11 slots (SF, SW; LR 10). **Fog of war**, all undead/monsters (FEW).
- **Field:** Thief (SH "for sure!", SW, LR Colm) and Torches (LR, SH). Light magic / Bishop (LR: "a
  Bishop is your best choice here", brings Natasha, Artur, Colm). A bow user with Antitoxin for the poison-
  bow Bonewalker (SW). FEW: "highly recommended to deploy promoted units such as Seth, or mounted units".
- L'Arachel (talk with Eirika) is fragile and the Deathgoyle group threatens her (SF, FEW).
- SH splits the party: Eirika + thief + average units south, strong units east.
- KE: after Dozla joins, "pick one or two [axe users among Ross, Gerik, Dozla] you like and bench the
  others."

## Chapter 12A: Village of Silence
- 12 slots (SF, SW; LR 11). **Saleh forced** (FEW). All monsters (Mauthe Doogs, Gargoyles, Bael...).
- **Field:** Bishop/light magic (LR, SH). Natasha, Artur (LR). Saleh visits the house for Ewan (SH; FEW
  says Ewan also joins at end if Saleh or Tethys alive).
- Use the map to level weak units, strong units front (SH). High-DEF unit parked 2 tiles from the boss to
  drain its Steel Bow (SH).
- KE: Saleh is "your best Anima and Light magic user" — bench Artur if training Saleh in light, Lute if
  in anima.

## Chapter 13A: Hamill Canyon
- 12 slots; survive 10-11 turns or kill Aias (SW says 10, SF/LR 11). Cavaliers T5/7/9, Cormag + 2 Wyvern
  Riders T5, Pablo T9 (SF, FEW, LR).
- **Field:** strong units (SH). Anti-horse weapons (LR). Eirika (+ axe users per SH) stays to talk to
  Cormag (all). Franz or Eirika for Amelia if she was missed in 9A (SW, SH, FEW). Bulky units survive the
  promoted Rangers (FEW). LP fielded Seth here because the bosses outclass the rest.
- Grounded lance unit in a forest to bait Pablo into the Hand Axe... (FEW, extraction unclear: likely
  "bait Pablo with a lance unit"); kill Pablo first (LR).

## Chapter 14A: Queen of White Dunes
- 12 slots; Knights, Snipers, Cavaliers, Shamans; Priest with Berserk; boss Carlyle (Swordmaster).
- **Field:** L'Arachel to recruit Rennac for free (SH, KE, LR, LP; Eirika must pay 9,980G, FEW). Colm for
  chests/doors (LR, LP). Natasha with Restore (LR). Cormag vs the Berserk priest (LR) and cavalier waves (LP).
- KE: bench Moulder if short on slots to fit L'Arachel.
- Carlyle: magic users at range; high-RES unit to break his Wind Sword; "ranged lances might not be
  super accurate" (FEW). **CONTESTED:** SH "Send in some lance-wielders and take down Carlyle".
