**When to promote.**
- **Casual and modern advice is level ~10-15**, because the game is short and the items are
  plentiful (R-1ST25: 12-14; GF-WCSIU: 10-15; SF-SETH L9999: ~13; R-snip hp6yet: even below
  15 is fine). Agree: 4.
- **The older "promote at 20" habit persists among first-timers:**
  - R-1ST13: save items until level 20.
  - R-snip 1cpirve: a first-timer promoted everyone at 20 except Joshua at 18.
  - SF-SETH OP: one of those "promote at 20" people.
- A bot imitating a casual player could use 20 when the next item isn't needed and the unit
  will plausibly get there, and 10-15 otherwise.

General rule of thumb: R-1ST25 says take the option with more movement. That is consistent
with the Paladin, Ranger, Mage Knight and Amelia-as-Cavalier picks.
