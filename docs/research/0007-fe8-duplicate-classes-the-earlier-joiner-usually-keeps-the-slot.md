# FE8 duplicate classes: the earlier joiner usually keeps the slot

Collected by web research for the #510 step 9 bot (2026-10-27). Paraphrased; source keys are record 0005's table; "agree" counts independent sources.

- **Cavaliers (Seth, Franz, Kyle, Forde; Amelia can go cavalier).**
  - Seth plus Franz are the default pair.
  - Kyle and Forde are optional third and fourth cavaliers. They are good when trained but
    don't catch up enough to justify stealing EXP from Franz (GF-WCSIU).
  - SF-COMP still rates them "shouldn't bench".
  - R-WHO: the player skipped Forde because Franz and Seth already filled the role.
  - **Typical field: 2-3 mounted lance or sword units** (Seth, Franz, plus one of Kyle, Forde
    or Duessel). Cavaliers are prized for movement (SF-COMP: "cavalry is busted").
  - Agree: 4.
- **Pegasus Knights (Vanessa, Tana, Syrene) plus Cormag.**
  - **Players field 1-2 fliers.** Vanessa is used early.
  - When Tana joins (Ch9-10), use both, then keep whichever has better stats by mid-game.
    A low-level Tana that matches Vanessa wins (GF-WCSIU).
  - SF-COMP: Vanessa's earlier availability usually wins, and Tana edges her statistically.
  - GF-CG: Tana is the best flier.
  - GF-QUICK: two fliers are redundant.
  - R-WHO: one commenter says keep Vanessa alongside Tana and Cormag; another says swap her
    out.
  - Syrene is almost always benched (SF-YATL D, SF-COMP bench).
  - **Agree on "at least one flier for rescue drops, villages, recruiting and objectives":**
    GF-WCSIU, GF-QUICK, R-WHO (late maps are about movement; fliers and Warp are at a
    premium), R-snip l2u79i, NL (5).
- **Knights (Gilliam; Amelia as Knight; Duessel and Kyle as Great Knight).**
  - **Usually 0-1 armour unit.** Gilliam is either committed to from the start or benched
    (SF-COMP).
  - Amelia is usually sent down the Cavalier line, not the Knight line (SF-PROMO 5 of 6).
  - Contrary voices: GF-CG (Amelia as a General is the best lance unit), NL (a usual tank is
    Amelia).
  - Walling is seen as weak in FE8 (SF-OLDTL).
  - Agree on "one armour at most": 4.
- **Archers (Neimi, Innes).**
  - **Field at most one bow unit.**
  - SF-NEIMI: Innes 10, Neimi 6. The split is conditional: Neimi with Tower grinding, Innes
    without.
  - GF-WCSIU: Neimi is outclassed on Eirika's route.
  - GF-CG prefers a Ranger Neimi.
  - R-WHO: Innes isn't needed late because fliers are scarce and mages handle wyverns.
  - Agree on Innes > Neimi for no-grind play: 4. Dissent: 2.
- **Healers (Moulder, Natasha, L'Arachel, later Saleh or promoted Artur/Lute as staff
  users).**
  - **1 dedicated healer, up to 2.** Promoted magic users take over staves later.
  - Moulder is the default because he joins first (GF-QUICK, SF-COMP, GF-WCSIU, GF-HEAL).
  - L'Arachel is used for horse mobility by a sizeable minority (GF-HEAL 4 of 10).
  - Natasha is often used as a Bishop fighter (GF-HEAL, R-1ST13).
  - GF-8v12: you don't need more than 2 healers.
  - GF-NOTOWER p1: a team without a dedicated healer was criticised.
  - Agree: 6.
- **Axe units (Ross, Garcia, Gerik, Dozla, Duessel).**
  - **Field one or two axe users.** GF-WCSIU names it a need, partly to wield Garm.
  - Gerik is the strongest pick on Eirika's route, Duessel on Ephraim's.
  - Ross or Garcia carry the early game. Ross is preferred long-term.
- **Swords (Eirika, Joshua, Colm, Marisa, Rennac).**
  - Avoid stacking sword-locked units, because swords lack ranged options.
  - Sources: GF-QUICK (use only one of Joshua or Colm), R-WHO (3 commenters say bench
    sword- or bow-locked units first), GF-CG (avoid 3 swordmasters).
  - Agree: 4.
- **Thieves (Colm, Rennac).** One thief. Colm is the default. Rennac appears only for steal or
  chest maps (GF-8v12, GF-CG, GF-NOTOWER). Agree: 3.
