# Baxby (bae_at1-flavour) Sword/Lance — imported axe-beak charge (#90 pipeline, per-character _u25).
# Frames: 000 idle · 001 stride(near) · 002 stride(far) · 003 hit(beak on the foe) · 004 dodge.
# The attack is a CHARGE: two strides across the gap -> the beak lands -> back up to home on foot.
# Frame position on the shared canvas IS the motion (see poses.yaml).
#
# CADENCE IS READ OFF `banim_bae_at1` (the Bael, FE8's ground-charging monster), not off the
# Cavalier donor and not off Lupin's wolf. A Bael crosses the screen ON ITS LEGS and walks BACK
# afterwards, which is exactly an axe-beak's shape of attack; a wolf's pounce leaves the ground
# and a horse's gallop-and-thrust is the very thing #206 exists to stop looking like. From it:
#   * a STEP sound fires on each stride of the run-up, and again on the way home;
#   * the approach ends on a LONG hold — the beat where the animal is committed but has not
#     struck — and that hold is what the crit lengthens;
#   * recovery walks BACKWARD through the run frames rather than snapping home.
#
# SOUND: NO HORSE SOUNDS ANYWHERE, deliberately. `sound_step_light_horse_*` and `sound_horse_neigh`
# are right there and would fit a Cavalier's rhythm perfectly — and would re-create the exact defect
# this animation exists to remove (a giant bird that sounds like a man on a horse). So:
#   * C1B `sound_step_heavy_quick` — a heavy two-legged footfall, one per stride.
#   * C42 `sound_axe_swing_long` — the beak coming round. He is an AXE-beak; the swing of a big
#     blade is the closest thing in the vanilla bank to what that head does, and it is the same
#     slot Bael punctuates with `sound_bael_1`/`_2` (a voice we do not have for a bird — vanilla's
#     only bird sound, C48, is the bard's SONGBIRD, which would read as comedy here).
#   * C21 `sound_hit_eliwood_promoted` — Bael's own impact SFX, not the wolf's C20.
# The cry is the one judgement here that cannot be checked by eye — confirm it by EAR on the bench.
#
# `attack_miss` is the attack body with ONLY the hit code + impact SFX removed — C04 and C01 STAY.
# Dropping C04 there is what soft-locked ch04 on turn 4 (#24 / decisions.md).
# When importing to FEditorAdv delete # and after each line.
