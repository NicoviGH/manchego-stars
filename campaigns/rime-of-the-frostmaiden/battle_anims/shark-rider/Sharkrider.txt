# the gap in two beats, the blow lands on the foe's near edge, and the mount backs home through

# so every C1B `sound_step_heavy_quick` is dropped and the surge is silent until the axe:
#   * C42 `sound_axe_swing_long` — literally an axe this time.
#   * C21 — Baxby's (and the Bael's) impact SFX.
# Timing is Baxby's unchanged, and every mode keeps its C04 / C01 pairing (the #24 soft-lock
# guard in feditor_to_banim checks it at import).
# When importing to FEditorAdv delete # and after each line.
