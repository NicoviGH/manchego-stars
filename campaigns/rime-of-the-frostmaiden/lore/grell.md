# Grell — the aberration in the Termalaine mine (ch03 boss) — Lore

## Profile

The grell is an aberration from the Underdark that came up the Termalaine mine's central shaft in
search of food. It has taken the deep workings as its lair and preys on miners and kobolds alike,
which is why the kobolds are so agitated.

It floats, it hunts alone, and it paralyzes what it catches with its tentacles. In battle it
fights as a Mogall, the floating eye of the original game.

## Book

In ''Rime of the Frostmaiden'' (p. 94), "a grell has infiltrated the mine, setting the kobolds on edge." From its cavern (p. 98) it stalks a lone victim, then "swoops in and tries to paralyze its prey, then hauls its catch back here to be devoured."
