# Messie — Lore & Voice

> The maer monster: an awakened plesiosaur in Maer Dualdon. ch06's cutscene actor, ch07's
> Speaker of Bremen. Faces: Syrene (no hat) and Gheb (the mayor's top hat), `portraits/messie.py`.
> He/him (Nicolas, 2026-08-26). Story frame: ADR 0332.

## Source canon

- *Rime* p.28: a frost druid's *awaken* spell gave him the Intelligence of an average person and
  Common. She expects him to attack Bremen's boats, and he obeys for fear of losing the gift.
- *Rime* p.31: his first words are "I'm listening." His own lines: "I used to just swim around
  the lake and eat fish." / "The human who gave me this gift is named Ravisin and claimed to serve
  the Frostmaiden..." / "I don't want to be a stupid beast again." A character can convince him
  the awakening is permanent, and he then promises to stop attacking fishers.
- DM notes: Marty spoke with him; the party proposed him for Speaker.

## Who he is (ours)

- **He has talked with the merfolk all along**, and he protests quietly: he rams Bremen's boats
  so the crews turn home before they reach merfolk water. He has never drowned anyone (ours; the
  book's Pronged Goat crew did drown, p.29). ADR 0332.
- He hides the protest behind obedience, because he fears Ravisin will take his mind back.
- ch06 ends with him going back to Bremen with the party to make peace. The Speaker idea comes
  out of ch07's argument with Dorbulgruf, and his first decree is a fish tax.

## Voice

He was an animal until a few months ago. Average intelligence, new to words.

**Diction rules**
- Short, plain, earnest sentences. He says exactly what he means. Closest vanilla register:
  Myrrh (MSG_A10/AAF), "No. I want to stay with you..."
- His world is the lake: fish, cold, water, ice and boats are his units of measure. This is
  also where ch07's fish tax comes from.
- He listens before he speaks, and he answers questions rather than making speeches.
- He says his fear plainly: "I don't want to be a stupid beast again."
- The prehistoric awe lives in how the others react to him (Braulo most of all). He never
  remarks on being ancient or grand.

**Calibration lines (book):** "I'm listening." · "I used to just swim around the lake and eat
fish." · "I don't want to be a stupid beast again."

**Banned:** roaring or growling text, oracle or riddle speech, "thee/thou", menace, cleverness
beyond a newly woken mind, and in ch06, knowing the Frostmaiden's plan (that is ch07's farewell).
