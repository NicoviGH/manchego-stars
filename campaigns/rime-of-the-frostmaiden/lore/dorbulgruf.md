# Dorbulgruf Shalescar — Lore & Voice

> Speaker of Bremen. Hires the party in ch06's opening; ch07's boss. Face: the Murray slot
> (`portraits/dorbulgruf.png`, ADR 0332). Story frame: ADR 0332.

## Source canon

- *Rime* p.27: Bremen's speaker and oldest living resident, a shield dwarf, "old even by dwarf
  standards." Lawful good. The book plays him as addled; **we do not** (Nicolas, 2026-10-09).
- DM notes: he refused to pay, claiming the party was never promised anything; Braulo swung first.

## Who he is (ours)

Grizzled, stubborn and angry. He has shared Maer Dualdon with its creatures all his life, and he
has decided that killing this one beast will fix Bremen's winter. Privately he is close to giving
in to Auril and starting the sacrifices the other towns make. He is frightened, and the fear comes
out as aggression: the more scared he is, the louder he gets.

## Voice

**Diction rules**
- Loud, short, exclamatory. Questions thrown back as accusations ("Heard, did you?").
- Talks over people and breaks off mid-sentence when the fear gets close ("Seventy years
  I've--" / "...Bah."). He covers every crack by shouting or changing the subject.
- Names the party by what annoys him ("this band of misfits"), never by a list of species.
- Gives in grudgingly and all at once ("...Fine! Three hundred gold.").
- Auril is never named. She surfaces as a slip ("a different way to pay her...") that he
  shouts over immediately.

**Calibration lines (locked, ch06 opening)**
- "Who let this band of misfits into my hall?"
- "Manchego Stars? Never heard of-- did that mushroom just SNEEZE on me?!"
- "...Then Bremen will have to find a different way to pay her..."

**Banned:** senility, drifting or memory gags (Nimsy owns the comic-old-Speaker lane, and the book's
addled read is retired), villain gloating or relish (he is a frightened man, never a villain),
comic greed or haggling shtick, saying Auril's name, calm.
