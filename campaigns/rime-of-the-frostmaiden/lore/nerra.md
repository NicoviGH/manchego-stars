# Nerra — Lore & Voice

> Elder of the Maer Dualdon merfolk; ch06's boss. Rides Novala's slot (`inject/cast.py`), face
> included: `portraits/nerra.png`, Freefall's Goth Serra repainted in the merfolk's own colours.
> Story frame: ADR 0332.

## Source canon

None. The merfolk, their unwritten coexistence with Bremen's fishers, and Auril emboldening them
are all ours (ADR 0332). Nerra's name is ours too.

## Who she is (ours)

The oldest and largest of the merfolk. For generations her people let Bremen's boats work the
lake. This winter someone promised them the fishermen, and she took the promise as a licence.
She enjoys it. When the fight turns, she saves herself: she retreats under the water instead
of dying on the ice, and she means to come back.

## Voice

**Shape:** the territorial elder, put out that the prey she was promised has teeth. She sits
apart from our other villains: Izobai counts coin, Sephek stays serene, Ravisin preaches. Nerra
is dry, contemptuous and self-interested.

**Diction rules**
- Dark irony in the FE8 villain register: she reframes the threat as a gift ("How generous").
- Her patron is "she", and never named. The Frostmaiden's plan is held for Messie's farewell
  in ch07 (ADR 0332).
- She speaks for herself. She does not give orders or explain her people.
- Never mentions Messie: the player meets the twist in his scene, not from her.

**Calibration lines (locked 2026-10-10)**
- Taunt (0x9EF's seat): "She said the fishermen were ours to take." / "Instead, Bremen sends
  this diverse bunch. How generous."
- Retreat (0x9F0's seat): "Ngh... Not today. The lake will have you another time..."

**Banned:** pleading, liturgy or Auril-reverence, naming the Frostmaiden, commanding the other
merfolk, and dying words. Her defeat is a retreat.
